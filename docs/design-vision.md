# Dashboard design vision

**Product style · Premium Aviation Editorial Intelligence**

The current design supersedes the original dark interface. Warm off-white pages and crisp white working surfaces frame a deep navy masthead and navigation. Georgia provides an editorial voice for page headings and the financial outcome; bundled Geist keeps controls, labels, and tabular numbers precise. Teal and blue identify the primary and comparison series. Terracotta marks adverse outcomes.

Use the composition of an executive research report: an integrated KPI strip, open narrative sections, focused chart surfaces, numbered insights, and a separate data-reliability audit. The Economics Lab is a financial decision workspace with a prominent modeled result, a three-step assumption workbench, scenario tiles, and a wide sensitivity matrix.

Chart units and legends must have reserved space. Tooltips retain counts, periods, and coverage. Missing observations remain missing. Every financial output is labeled as estimated or modeled and remains tied to explicit assumptions. Stable carrier colors, line styles, and readable labels support comparison without relying on color alone.

Desktop and laptop layouts are primary. Paired charts stack below 1000px. On phones, native controls wrap, KPIs form two columns, and dense figures scroll within their own panels so labels remain readable. Hover, selection and keyboard focus use native Streamlit behavior with subtle CSS feedback and reduced-motion support.
